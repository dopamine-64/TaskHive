#

TaskHive is a **Laravel-based service marketplace platform** that connects customers with service providers. It supports service discovery, booking, payments, real-time tracking, ratings, complaints and administrative management.

## Features

### 🔐 Authentication & Authorization
- User registration and login
- OTP verification
- Role-based access for customers, providers and admins
- Secure authentication and authorization

### 🛠️ Service Management
- Service providers can manage their services
- Services can be categorized and searched
- Admins can edit, enable/disable and delete services
- Customers can browse available services and providers

### 📅 Booking System
- Customers can book services from providers
- Bookings can be rescheduled or cancelled
- Providers can accept or decline bookings
- Booking status can be updated throughout the service process

### 💳 Payment & Invoices
- Online payment initiation
- Success, failure and cancellation handling
- Wallet-based payments
- Invoice viewing after payment

### 📍 Real-Time Tracking
- Providers can start and manage tracking for active bookings
- Customers can track their ongoing bookings
- Providers can accept, decline and complete tracking sessions

### ⭐ Ratings & Reviews
- Customers can rate service providers after completing a booking
- Reviews can be managed or deleted when permitted

### 📢 Complaint Management
- Customers can submit complaints about services
- Admins can view, update and delete complaints

### 👨‍💼 Admin Panel
- Manage users and service providers
- Verify or remove providers
- Manage bookings and services
- Handle customer complaints
- View reports and system activities

### 👤 Profile Management
- Customers and providers can manage their profiles
- Providers have dedicated dashboards for managing their services and bookings

### 🔮 Future Improvements
- Enhanced real-time location tracking
- Push notifications
- Improved provider discovery
- Advanced search and filtering
- More detailed analytics
- Automated booking reminders
- Expanded payment options
- Mobile application
- Improved recommendation system
- More comprehensive automated testing
- API expansion for mobile/frontend clients
